Join Our Volunteer Team

Volunteers are the heart of our mission. Since the start of the Syrian crisis, dedicated volunteers from around the world have come together to provide help, hope, and healing.

Whether you're a student, doctor, teacher, or simply someone who wants to help — there's a place for you in our family. You can assist in campaigns, organize events, or support us online by spreading awareness.

Interested in volunteering? Send us an email at volunteer@hopeforsyrianchildren.org or visit Molham Team Volunteer Page to learn more.
