About Us

This website supports and promotes humanitarian organizations such as Molham Volunteering Team, who are making a real impact on the lives of Syrian children and families.

Established by a group of Syrian university students in 2012, Molham Team began as a grassroots effort to help displaced Syrians across neighboring countries. Over time, it grew into an international nonprofit with legal offices in Turkey, Germany, Sweden, and Canada.

Today, their programs focus on:

  • Orphan Sponsorship: Over 3,500 orphaned children are supported with food, education, clothing, and healthcare.
  • Education Programs: Operating schools and scholarships across Syria and refugee areas, including Molham Orphan School and Molham Village School.
  • Medical & Family Sponsorships: Helping families with long-term needs and children with special medical conditions.
  • Molham Events: A unique way for donors to share their own celebrations (like weddings or birthdays) by bringing joy to displaced families in refugee camps.

Through this site, we aim to amplify their efforts and raise awareness of the real, ongoing humanitarian needs — and how anyone can help.
